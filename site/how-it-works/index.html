From first question to final review.

A practical operating sequence. The free pricing calculator works today; the connected consulting application is planned.

Consulting platform: in development

The consulting workflow is planned and not available as a hosted customer service. These pages describe the intended direction, not working app features.

  1. Capture the client problem

    You enter: You record the question, context and decision-maker.

    Clariro Systems’s role: The planned workflow would connect a client with intake notes.

    Your judgment: You decide whether the problem is accurately understood.

  2. Qualify the opportunity

    You enter: You record needs, constraints and fit.

    Clariro Systems’s role: The planned workflow would organize qualification notes.

    Your judgment: You decide whether to accept or decline the work.

  3. Define scope and deliverables

    You enter: You specify outcomes, exclusions, milestones and costs.

    Clariro Systems’s role: Today’s calculator estimates a quote; the planned app would link scope and deliverables.

    Your judgment: You agree scope, price and acceptance terms with the client.

  4. Build the work plan

    You enter: You break the scope into tasks and dependencies.

    Clariro Systems’s role: The planned workflow would connect work items to the engagement.

    Your judgment: You choose the approach, owners and realistic deadlines.

  5. Organize evidence

    You enter: You identify sources, observations and limitations.

    Clariro Systems’s role: The planned workflow would link evidence references to findings.

    Your judgment: You assess relevance, accuracy, confidentiality and sufficiency.

  6. Produce the deliverable

    You enter: You write findings and recommendations.

    Clariro Systems’s role: The planned workflow would connect deliverables with supporting work.

    Your judgment: You review quality and take responsibility for professional conclusions.

  7. Track time and expenses

    You enter: You record actual hours and attributable costs.

    Clariro Systems’s role: The planned workflow would organize time and expense records.

    Your judgment: You determine billing and appropriate reporting treatment.

  8. Review and follow up

    You enter: You compare results against the agreed baseline.

    Clariro Systems’s role: The planned workflow would connect review notes and next actions.

    Your judgment: You interpret the variance and agree useful follow-up.
