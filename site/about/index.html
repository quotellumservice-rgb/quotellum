About Quotellum

Practical tools. Clear assumptions.

Quotellum helps independent consultants and service businesses reason about scope, price and project contribution.

What is available

The public offering is a free browser quote calculator, a free Excel sample and educational resources. The calculator runs on your device without an account or project upload.

What we are building

A proposed $15 one-time Quote Kit is in development and certification. A broader consulting workflow is planned. Neither is available for purchase or as a hosted customer application.
