Privacy and data handling

Updated 2026-10-17 · Applies to the current free public website. Draft commercial terms are explicitly marked and need owner/legal/tax review before paid launch.

Calculator inputs

The free calculator runs in your browser. The controller does not submit inputs, write cookies, use browser storage or contact a service. Your entered figures exist in page memory until reset, refresh or closing the page. This statement does not describe a future app.

Ordinary website requests

Netlify serves these static pages and files. Visiting the site or downloading the sample sends ordinary web-request information to the hosting provider, such as IP address, requested path and browser information. Provider handling and retention are governed by its services; this site does not promise a provider-log deletion period.

Analytics, accounts and payments

This public build includes no analytics SDK, advertising pixels, account signup, payment collection or project-upload service. No calculator input analytics are collected by this build. We make no blanket claim that hosting providers collect no operational data.

Support email

If you email quotellum.service@gmail.com, your message and contact details are handled in the support mailbox. Email is not a secure file intake channel. Avoid confidential, privileged or sensitive client information. This build does not implement an automated email-retention or deletion schedule.

Retention and deletion requests

There are no calculator projects or user accounts stored by this site to delete. You can reset the calculator or close the page. For a support-email deletion request, contact the same address and identify the message without sending additional sensitive data. Requests require manual review and may be subject to applicable obligations or provider limits.

Future services

Hosted accounts, paid delivery and shared workspaces would require an updated privacy notice before activation. No future data-processing behavior is represented as available here.
