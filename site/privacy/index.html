Privacy and data handling

Updated 2026-10-18 · Applies to the current free public website. Draft commercial terms are explicitly marked and need owner/legal/tax review before paid launch.

Calculator inputs

Calculations run on your device. Inputs are not sent to Clariro. The calculator offers optional Save on this device for one estimate, stored under clariro.free.estimate.v1. Saving replaces the prior estimate on that browser. Load and Delete saved estimate are explicit controls; resetting the example does not delete a saved record. JSON backups are downloaded locally and validated before restore. Browser storage and backup files are not encrypted. Avoid confidential client information and shared-device storage. Blocking storage does not prevent calculating or downloading.

Ordinary website requests

Netlify serves these static pages and files. Visiting the site or downloading the sample sends ordinary web-request information to the hosting provider, such as IP address, requested path and browser information. Provider handling and retention are governed by its services; this site does not promise a provider-log deletion period.

Analytics, accounts and payments

This public build includes no analytics SDK, advertising pixels, account signup, payment collection or project-upload service. No calculator input analytics are collected by this build. We make no blanket claim that hosting providers collect no operational data.

Support email

If you email Clariro support, your message and contact details are handled in the support mailbox. Email is not a secure file intake channel. Avoid confidential, privileged or sensitive client information. This build does not implement an automated email-retention or deletion schedule.

Retention and deletion requests

There are no cloud project accounts. Use Delete saved estimate to remove the optional browser record. Reset clears the form but keeps the saved record. Delete exported backup files yourself when no longer needed. For a support-email deletion request, contact the same address and identify the message without sending additional sensitive data. Requests require manual review and may be subject to applicable obligations or provider limits.

Future services

Hosted accounts, paid delivery and shared workspaces would require an updated privacy notice before activation. No future data-processing behavior is represented as available here.
