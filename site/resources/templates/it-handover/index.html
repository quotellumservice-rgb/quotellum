IT consulting handover checklist

Confirm ownership, documentation and recovery before closing an IT engagement.

Audience: IT consultants handing work to a client team
Last updated: 2026-10-18

Scope and change record

List delivered changes, affected systems, approved exceptions, outstanding issues and the authorized acceptance contact.

Operating information

Document runbooks, expected behavior, support boundaries, escalation contacts and maintenance responsibilities. Transfer secrets through an approved credential system, never in a public checklist.

Validation and recovery

Record tests actually run, relevant results, backup responsibilities and the rollback or recovery method. An untested recovery plan should be labeled untested.

Acceptance

Agree the handover date, open actions, owners and follow-up. This template does not monitor systems, execute migrations or replace enterprise IT service-management tools.
