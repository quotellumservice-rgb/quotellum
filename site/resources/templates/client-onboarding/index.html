Client onboarding checklist

Align contacts, access, scope and next steps before delivery starts.

Audience: Consultants preparing a new client relationship
Last updated: 2026-10-17

People and purpose

Confirm client contacts, decision-maker, billing contact, business problem and desired outcome. Collect only the information needed for the engagement.

Scope and access

Confirm the agreed work, exclusions, access approvals, information owner, private file channel and client dependencies. Avoid putting confidential information into shared demonstrations.

Working rhythm

Agree a kickoff, update frequency, decisions log, review windows and escalation method. Record how a scope change is requested and accepted.

Ready to begin

Check commercial acceptance, required professional checks, initial information and the first milestone. These are planning prompts, not a substitute for applicable contractual duties.

Use this resource

Read, copy the prompts for your own business, or use your browser’s Print command to keep a copy. There is no separate downloadable asset for this page.
