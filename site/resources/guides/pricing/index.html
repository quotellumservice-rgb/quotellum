Price a service engagement

Build a cost floor, distinguish margin from markup, and test the assumptions behind a quote.

Audience: Solo consultants and service-business owners
Last updated: 2026-10-18

Start with delivery cost

List materials, billable delivery hours at your internal hourly cost, project overhead and other direct costs. An internal hourly cost is not the rate you charge a client.

Calculate a margin-based price

For a $220 delivery cost and a 30% target margin before fees, divide $220 by 0.70. The result rounds to $314.29. Adding 30% to cost instead gives $286 and only about 23.1% margin.

Allow for omitted costs

The free calculator excludes processing fees, refunds, acquisition and support. Deduct those obligations when deciding whether the customer price is acceptable. Taxes collected for authorities are not contribution.

Review before sending

Check assumptions, scope, exclusions, payment timing and the client’s acceptance process. Compare a lower and higher price against the same delivery cost. A calculated price is a planning input, not proof of demand.
