Understand project contribution

Separate revenue, direct project costs and cash movement before judging a project.

Audience: Service businesses reviewing completed work
Last updated: 2026-10-17

Define the measure

Project contribution is project revenue less the costs you choose to attribute to delivering and acquiring that work. Document the definition so projects remain comparable.

Use a small worked example

On $500 revenue, subtract $250 delivery cost, $15 processing cost, $20 acquisition cost and $10 support allowance. Estimated contribution is $205, or 41% of revenue. This illustration is not a recorded Quotellum business result.

Keep cash separate

An upfront deposit changes cash timing; it does not automatically mean all work is earned or delivered. A project can have positive contribution while still needing cash to cover an earlier outlay.

Close the loop

Compare estimates to actual hours and costs, record the reason for a variance, and change one pricing or scoping assumption for the next engagement. Your accountant determines financial reporting treatment.
