What the example tells you

$100 materials + 4 hours × $25 hourly cost + $20 overhead = $220 delivery cost. A 30% target margin before fees gives $220 ÷ 0.70 = $314.29, rounded to cents. The comparison varies that price by −10%, 0% and +10%; it does not change the delivery assumptions.
