How we calculated this

Starting price = (job costs + fixed payment fee) ÷ (1 − percentage goal − percentage fee). Payment fees are rounded to cents at each price. The cost-covering price uses a zero percentage goal. Results may differ by a cent because of rounding.

Check before you send the quote

  • Write down scope, deliverables, exclusions and revision limits.
  • Include preparation, meetings, travel and administration.
  • Confirm costs, payment timing and any deposit or milestone terms.
  • Allow separately for refunds, acquiring the customer, ongoing support and tax obligations.
  • Check demand and your capacity. A positive estimate does not guarantee a good engagement.
