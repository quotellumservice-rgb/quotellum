FORMULA: LABOR
Total modeled hours = job hours + travel/meetings/admin hours + contingency hours. Labor cost = total modeled hours × hourly cost, rounded to cents.

FORMULA: PRICE AND COSTS
Customer price = planned price × (1 − discount), rounded to cents. Delivery cost = labor + materials + helpers + travel + other + allocated business costs.

FORMULA: REMAINING
Total estimated costs = delivery cost + percentage processing fee + fixed processing fee + planning reserve. Each percentage allowance is rounded independently to cents. Remaining = customer price − total estimated costs.

FORMULA: GOALS
Margin = remaining ÷ customer price. Break-even = (delivery costs + fixed fee) ÷ (1 − fee rate − reserve rate). Goal price = same numerator ÷ (1 − goal margin − fee rate − reserve rate), rounded up to cents. Actual displayed margin can differ slightly because fees round independently.

FORMULA: HOURLY RESULT
Remaining per modeled hour = remaining ÷ total modeled hours. Your labor cost has already been deducted. This is not a wage or a cash-flow forecast. Zero hours or zero price produces n.a. for the corresponding ratio.

MISSING VERSUS ZERO
Required fields left blank produce unavailable results. 0 is a valid explicit assumption. Negative inputs, unsupported text, discount ≥100%, or goal + fee + reserve ≥100% are invalid. Do not bypass validation.

CHECKLIST: SCOPE
Write scope, deliverables, exclusions and revision limits. Include meetings, travel, administration and expected rework.

CHECKLIST: COMMERCIAL TERMS
Confirm costs and fees; agree payment timing, deposit/milestones, acceptance and change requests. Confirm tax obligations separately before sending a quote.

GLOSSARY: REMAINING AMOUNT
Estimated money left after only the modeled costs and allowances. It is not verified profit, tax advice or guaranteed earnings.

GLOSSARY: MARGIN / MARKUP
Margin divides remaining amount by selling price. Markup divides the uplift by cost. $100 cost plus 30% markup gives $130, not a 30% margin.

GLOSSARY: BUSINESS COSTS
A fair share of operating costs assigned to this job. Do not also include the same costs in hourly cost or another expense.

GLOSSARY: BREAK-EVEN
The modeled customer price at which remaining is approximately zero. Omitted costs can make the real break-even higher.
