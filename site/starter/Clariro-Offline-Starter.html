Optional blanks mean zero. Do not count any cost twice. Percentages are your assumptions; reserves are not tax due.

How we calculated this

Target-price estimates round upward to cents; percentage fee and reserve round independently. Remaining per hour is after your modeled labor cost, so it is not your wage. Tax and omitted obligations are excluded. A deposit changes cash timing, not earned revenue.

Before sending your quote

  • Confirm costs, applicable fees and separate tax obligations.
