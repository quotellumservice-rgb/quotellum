Home / Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered plainly.

What can I use today?

The free calculator, the free Excel sample and the static educational resources. No login is needed.

Can I buy the $15 Quote Kit?

No. The Quote Kit is still being tested and checkout is closed. No payment is accepted on this site.

Does the calculator save my projects?

No. Inputs remain in the page’s memory, with no local project storage or server submission. Refresh or Reset example clears the current values.

What does contribution mean here?

The free result is quoted price minus modeled delivery cost. It excludes processing fees, refunds, acquisition, support and other business obligations. It is not net business profit.

Is the consulting application available?

No. The consulting platform and vertical packs are planned and not available for purchase. Descriptive workflow pages are not a live app.

Does the site provide professional advice?

No. Estimates and checklists support your decisions and do not replace qualified legal, accounting, tax, investment or professional judgment.

Can I recover a purchase or request a refund?

Paid purchases are not open. Recovery and refund workflows are not offered as live services on this public site. The linked intended commercial policies remain drafts.

How can I report a security concern?

Email our support team with a minimal non-sensitive description. Do not access others’ data or send secrets.
