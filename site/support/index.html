Questions about the free calculator, a broken page or the sample file? Email Quotellum.

Email support

Help us understand the issue

  • Include the page address, browser or Excel version and what you expected.
  • Describe a reproducible example using fictional values.
  • Do not send passwords, payment card details, client records, legal documents or confidential project information.

Email opens your own mail application. This site has no support form or automatic support ticket service. No response-time guarantee is offered.

Try these steps first

  • Refresh the page and use Reset example to restore calculator defaults.
  • Check that values are nonnegative and use the supported decimal precision.
  • Check that target margin is below 100%.
  • For the free workbook, use a compatible spreadsheet application; Microsoft Excel is not included.

Intended paid-product boundaries

The Quote Kit is not for sale. Its intended support is 30 days of setup and file troubleshooting from purchase, with 12 calendar months of purchase-download recovery. Those services require completed certification and final owner approval. Custom consulting, professional advice, custom development and indefinite updates are not included.
